A little about me.

“

I like the part where a diagram becomes a device.

I design closed-loop systems where the hard part is often the sensor, not the algorithm — from gyro-stabilised navigation and acoustic communication to telemetry and embedded control.

My approach is practical: define the constraints, model the system, build the prototype, measure it, then iterate until the hardware behaves the way it should.
